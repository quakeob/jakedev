How it works

From inbox to keepsake, automatically.

You set it up once. After that, Missionary Moments does the remembering.

✉️
STEP 01

Mom sets it up

Pick a theme, add your missionary's email, and invite family members who want updates by text or email.

STEP 02

We send the prompt

Each week your missionary gets one thoughtful question — answerable by email, voice recording, or video.

STEP 03

They just hit reply

Their answer is published straight to their personal website. They get a confirmation; you get a notification.

STEP 04

The family follows along

Grandparents, siblings, and friends visit one beautiful page — photos, voice memos, videos, and milestones.
