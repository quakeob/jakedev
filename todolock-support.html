These instructions cover the 1.2 update. If your installed version is 1.1, app rules are in Settings; the new Plan and Focus tabs appear after updating.

Start with one task

In TodoLock 1.2, setup starts with a daily task. Choose Repeat every day if it is a routine. You can allow Screen Time and choose apps during setup, or choose Use without blocking and enable it later from Focus. Notifications are optional.

Plan holds goals, daily tasks, and weekly to-dos. Only daily tasks count toward your app-unlock quota. Goals and weekly tasks record progress without unlocking apps.

Choose your app rules in Focus

  • Daily task lock: choose apps and a daily quota. Complete that many daily tasks to end this rule's lock.
  • Super Lock: choose a separate app list, weekdays, and a time window of at least 15 minutes. Tasks do not end a scheduled lock. An overnight window belongs to the day it starts.
  • 20 minute mode: use the selected apps for 20 minutes in total, then take a 2 minute break. Switching between selected apps does not reset the allowance. The next session becomes available after the break. Requires iOS 17.4 or later.

Each of these rules has its own app selection. Night Mode uses your daily task lock's selection. Vacation Mode pauses daily and night rules until the chosen date; Super Lock and 20 minute mode remain active.

Why is an app still blocked?

Check all active rules in Focus. Meeting your daily quota does not end Super Lock, Night Mode, or a 2 minute break. Other restrictions in Apple's Screen Time settings can also apply.

In Strict mode, the configured daily quota still applies if your list has fewer tasks. A completion cooldown can delay the next check-off. TodoLock's default daily reset is 4:00 a.m.; an earned daily unlock lasts until the next app day.

Screen Time can deliver scheduled transitions late. If a rule shows an error, follow its retry instructions or reopen TodoLock. Revoking Screen Time access prevents TodoLock from enforcing its rules.

How do timed sessions work?

The allowance measures use across your chosen apps and websites, not time since you enabled the mode. After 20 minutes of use, the selection is blocked for 2 minutes. The next usage session must be registered before TodoLock clears that break. Other active rules may continue to block an app. A session resets after 24 hours if its allowance was not used.

How does snooze work?

Reminder mode can offer a short snooze while your daily quota is unmet. It is limited to once per hour and is unavailable during Night Mode. Snoozing does not bypass Super Lock or a timed break. After the shield closes, tap the original app icon again. Lock now ends an active daily snooze early.

How do tasks repeat?

Enable daily repetition when creating a task, or use the task's edit controls. Repeating tasks return on the next app day. Use This week in Plan for weekly to-dos; these track progress without affecting the daily unlock quota.

What does Progress show?

Progress contains completed-task history, calendar activity, streaks, and achievements. One completed daily task earns an activity day. Goals and weekly tasks record their own progress. Snooze time describes requested breaks; it is not a measurement of other-app usage.

How does Friends sharing work?

Friends requires your iCloud account. Enable sharing to publish your chosen name, current and best streaks, today's daily completion count, and update information. Task titles and app selections are not shared.

Invitations share in one direction. Accepting a friend's invitation does not share your progress back. Manage access controls who can see you; Stop following removes an incoming share. Stop sharing removes your profile and invitations after iCloud confirms deletion.

On a new phone, TodoLock can show your existing profile. Use this phone for updates makes that installation the source of your shared progress. Histories are not merged between phones. Keep every publishing device updated. If iCloud is unavailable, the app labels cached information and offers retry controls.

Theme, motion, and widgets

Choose Forma or Classic in Settings. Forma includes animated ASCII artwork and button feedback. Both support larger text and the iPhone's Reduce Motion setting. Settings also lets you replay What's new.

iOS controls widget refresh timing. Open TodoLock to refresh task state, then allow time for the widget to update.

Permissions and removing data

Change Screen Time access and notification permissions in iPhone Settings. Planning works without blocking permission, and notifications are optional.

Tasks and settings are stored locally. Deleting a task removes it from the active list while completed history can remain. Delete TodoLock rather than offloading it to remove local app data; manage backups separately. Deleting the app does not delete a shared iCloud profile: use Stop sharing first.
