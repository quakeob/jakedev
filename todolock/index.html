A new home for your day

Four tabs: Plan, Focus, Progress, and Friends.

  • [×]

    Plan

    Keep goals at the top of your day. Add daily tasks, weekly to-dos, and repeating routines. Only daily tasks count toward unlocking apps.

  • [×]

    Focus

    Choose apps to lock until your daily quota is complete. Super Lock runs on weekday or overnight schedules. 20 minute mode gives 20 minutes of use, then a 2 minute break.

  • [×]

    Progress

    Your completed tasks, calendar activity, streaks, and achievements. One completed daily task earns an activity day.

  • [×]

    Friends

    Choose whether to share a small progress summary through iCloud: your chosen name, your streaks, and today's completed-task count.

Forma adds moving ASCII artwork and touch feedback. Classic remains available.
