Questions brands ask

What kinds of brands do you work with?
Outdoor brands, mostly camping, overlanding, ski and travel. If it gets used on a real trip, it's a good fit: stoves, fire pits, griddles, coolers, tents and shells.
What's included in a brand deal or UGC package?
Vertical video from 15 to 60 seconds, delivered as a finished edit plus the raw clips, and stills, either straight out of camera or graded to match your feed. A typical package is one main video, a few short cutdowns and a set of photos.
What are your rates?
Rates depend on the package. Email jake@bryt.works and I'll send them along with the full media kit.
Can I commission content for my brand's own channels?
Yes. Tell me whether you need photos and video for your website, social channels or ads, a sponsored post on @jjakedavis, or both. We'll agree on deliverables, usage, posting and timing for the project.
How are usage rights and raw clips handled?
The video offer includes a finished edit plus raw clips. Include your intended channels, paid advertising, usage period and any exclusivity in the brief so those terms can be agreed in the quote.
Where do you shoot?
On real trips, never in a studio: camp in Utah's West Desert, ski-lot mornings at Snowbasin and Beaver Mountain, and trips from the Tetons and Southeast Alaska to Switzerland and Lapland. Your product rides along in a 1998 4Runner, a 5th gen 4Runner or a custom Sprinter.
How big is your audience?
About 4M views a month across my photo and video work on Instagram, at @jjakedavis (Nov 2026).
Where are you based?
Salt Lake City, Utah. Most shoots happen around Utah and the Mountain West, with trips further afield through the year.
How do we get started?
Email jake@bryt.works with the product, your dates, location, the photos or videos you need and where the content will run. I'll send rates and the full media kit so we can agree on the scope.
