TodoLock Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 16, 2026
Developer: Jake Davis
Contact: jake@bryt.works

What we collect

TodoLock stores your task list, settings, and progress locally on your iPhone. It has no account service or backend that receives this information.

TodoLock stores all of its data on your iPhone:

  • Your task list (titles, completion times, recurrence settings)
  • Your daily stats (streak, total completions, snooze counts)
  • Your settings (blocked apps, mode, quota, schedule)
  • The apps and categories you choose to shield

The app does not upload this information to the developer. It includes no advertising, third-party analytics, third-party crash reporter, or tracking across apps and websites. There is no TodoLock cloud-sync service.

TodoLock shares relevant local information with its own extensions through an Apple App Group. Pending task titles may appear on a blocking screen or widget. Consider who can see your screen when choosing task names or adding a Lock Screen widget.

What we use Apple frameworks for

TodoLock requests permission to use Apple's Family Controls / Screen Time API to shield apps you choose. Apple provides privacy-preserving tokens for your selected apps and categories. TodoLock stores and uses these tokens locally to apply your blocking choices; it does not send your selections to the developer.

TodoLock also uses:

  • SwiftData for your task list (stored locally)
  • UserDefaults for settings and daily state (stored locally)
  • DeviceActivity for blocking schedules and daily resets
  • UserNotifications for optional local banners that help you return to TodoLock or explain a snooze. No TodoLock push-notification server is involved.

You can revoke TodoLock's Screen Time access and change notification permissions in iPhone Settings. Revoking Screen Time access prevents TodoLock from enforcing your selected blocking rules.

Apple may process information when you use the App Store, system diagnostics, or device backups under your Apple settings and Apple's privacy policies. Device backups may contain local app data. These system services are separate from TodoLock's own data handling.

Retention and deletion

Your local information remains on your device while the app is installed. Deleting a task removes it from the active list; completed history can remain locally for your stats. TodoLock has no server copy for the developer to retrieve or delete.

To remove TodoLock's local app data, delete the app rather than offloading it. Manage existing backups separately in your Apple or computer backup settings. Restoring a backup may restore data included in it.

Support and website visits

If you choose to email jake@bryt.works, your email provider sends your address, message, and any attachments to the developer to respond to your request. Avoid including private task content unless necessary to explain the issue. Support correspondence is retained only as needed to handle the request and meet applicable obligations; you can request deletion by email.

Opening the privacy or support website uses your browser and the website's hosting service. Ordinary website requests may include information such as your IP address. Local app storage does not mean that email and web browsing happen entirely on your device.

Children

TodoLock is a personal productivity app and is not directed to children under 13. The app does not collect personal information from users. If you believe a child has sent personal information in a support message, contact the developer to request its removal.

Changes

This page will be updated if TodoLock's privacy practices change. The effective date above identifies the current policy.
