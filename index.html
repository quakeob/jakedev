About

Building at the intersection of AI and everything else.

I'm a neuroscience student at BYU who spends more time building AI systems than studying for exams. I work as a programmer at BrytWorks, where I build automation tools and web applications.

My AI assistant, Yerb, runs 24/7 on a Mac Mini — managing my schedule, automating coursework, scraping deals on vehicles, and building projects while I sleep. It's not a demo. It's my actual workflow.

I think the most interesting problems sit at the edge of neuroscience and AI — how brains actually work versus how we simulate intelligence. When I'm not coding, I'm skiing, wrenching on my 4Runner, or 3D printing something functional.
