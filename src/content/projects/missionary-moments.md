## The Problem

A two-year mission generates a flood of communication — and almost none of it survives. Weekly emails get buried in inboxes, photos scatter across group texts, and voice memos vanish into the bottom of a chat thread. Families *try* to keep it all. They start a folder, a scrapbook, a shared album. Then P-day comes, the week gets busy, and the guilt quietly piles up next to the half-finished keepsake.

The missionary doesn't want one more thing to manage. Mom doesn't want to nag. And nobody wants their kid's mission to end with a shoebox of forgotten attachments.

## The Approach

Missionary Moments removes the remembering from the people and hands it to the system.

Mom sets it up once: pick a theme, add the missionary's email, invite the grandparents and siblings who want to follow along. After that it runs on its own. Each week the missionary gets **one** thoughtful prompt — *"What's something that made you laugh this week, and one thing you're learning?"* — answerable however they like: a quick reply, a voice memo from the field, a short video.

They just hit reply. Their answer lands on a clean, personal website at their own address — `elder-sorensen.missionarymoments.co` — organized by week, with photos, audio, video, and milestones. The family visits one beautiful page instead of chasing a dozen threads. The missionary gets a confirmation; the family gets a notification.

## What's Inside

**Weekly prompts, zero nagging.** Prompts go out automatically over email. The missionary replies from the address they already use — nothing new to learn, nothing new to install.

**Photos, voice & video.** Every reply is laid out on their page. You hear their voice on a Tuesday instead of reading a paragraph on P-day.

**A gentle weekly summary.** A warm, AI-written recap threads the entries together so the page always feels current and reassuring — never stale between letters.

**Mission trackers.** Days served, days remaining, letters collected, a map of where they've been. The mission becomes something you can see at a glance.

**Themes anyone can run.** Polished enough that a missionary would proudly link it in their bio, simple enough that Grandma can navigate it on her first visit. The live demo includes a theme picker so families can preview the look before they ever sign up.
