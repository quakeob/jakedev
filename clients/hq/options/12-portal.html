The House

Rules that protect the room.

Confidentiality

What happens in the room stays there. An absolute no-photo policy ensures a true sanctuary.

Discretion

Members exercise judgment in all things. The club's reputation is the sum of each member's conduct.

Conduct

The standard is not a rulebook. It is an expectation — written, signed, and understood before entry.

The club is a place, not a platform. Staff are professional, discreet, and invisible when needed.
