Questions worth asking

Before you buy.

What is in one serving?

Two capsules provide a proprietary 800 mg probiotic blend listed at 40 billion CFU, plus 60 mg of complex marine polysaccharide and 24 mg of fructooligosaccharide.

Are the individual strain amounts listed?

No. The supplier names all four strains but provides one combined amount for the probiotic blend. We do not estimate or invent the individual allocations.

Is this a treatment for an infection?

No. This is a dietary supplement. If you suspect an infection or have severe, persistent, or worsening symptoms, get evaluated by a qualified healthcare professional.
