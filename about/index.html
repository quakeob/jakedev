I build software and make photographs and films. The work covers web development, UI and UX design, automation, photography and filmmaking, and I like making things that work well and feel considered.

My photo and video work has grown to around 4 million views a month. The places get noticed, but what I'm really working on is how they're captured: composition, pacing, and the balance between simplicity and detail, without letting it feel overdone.

With software, I work closely with the people who'll use it. I learn the problem, map how the tools should connect, design the interface, then build and improve it around real feedback. For clients I stay close to the day-to-day work, so the software fits the process and not the other way round. Most of it is workflow automation, AI agents and web apps, built around clear flows and practical business needs.

Right now I'm building Forma Nova, a design-focused AI development tool, and Pholio, an expressive portfolio and link-in-bio for creators. Both bring my technical and creative work together.
